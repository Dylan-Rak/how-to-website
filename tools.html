Nipper

A Nipper, also known as a Clipper, is the quintessential tool in your tool kit.
it is your primary means of detatching parts from a runner. your nipper can take
many forms but double bladed and single bladed are the most common.
single bladed nippers are often considered superior in persision and making
clean cuts but are also more delecate, the God Hand SPN-120 is often credited as
best single bladed nipper on the market.
